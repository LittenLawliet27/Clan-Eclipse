What We Stand For

Core Values

Clan Eclipse isn't about being the biggest, loudest, or most popular group. It's about creating a community where people can belong, contribute, and be themselves.

01 / UNITY

Together

We bring people together instead of dividing them. Everyone has something different to contribute to Eclipse.

02 / RESPECT

Respect

Every member deserves to be treated with respect. We build each other up rather than tearing each other down.

03 / INDIVIDUALITY

Be Yourself

You don't need to change who you are to belong here. Being different is part of what makes our community unique.

04 / KINDNESS

Look Out For Others

A community is only as strong as the way its members treat one another. Kindness matters.

05 / COMMUNITY

Build Together

We encourage collaboration, friendships, student projects, activities, and positive connections around school.

06 / PURPOSE

Make a Difference

We're students, but that doesn't mean we can't make an impact. Small actions can make our school community better.

You don't have to fit in to find somewhere you belong.
