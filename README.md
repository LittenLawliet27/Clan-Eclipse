#  Clan Eclipse

> **Bringing people together. Building a community. Making a difference.**

**Clan Eclipse** is a student-led community founded and owned by **Aaron Gonzalez**, also known as **Cherry Blossom / Ash Silver**.

Clan Eclipse was created with one simple goal: **bring people together.** Whether you're a misfit, someone looking for a group to belong to, or simply someone who wants to meet new people, Eclipse is meant to be a place where students can connect, hang out, collaborate, and support one another.

## 🌘 Our Mission

Clan Eclipse is about more than just having a group name. We're working toward building a positive student community that can:

* 🤝 Bring students from different groups together
* 🌎 Create a welcoming environment for people who may feel left out
* 💬 Encourage friendships, communication, and collaboration
* 🏫 Organize official group meetings around school
* 🎯 Work on student-led projects and activities
* 💜 Support members and look out for one another
* ✨ Make a positive impact within our school community

As high school students, we may not be able to change everything—but **we can still make a difference where we are.**

## 🌑 Who Can Join?

**Anyone is welcome to join Clan Eclipse** as long as they are willing to respect others and contribute positively to the community.

You don't have to be popular.

You don't have to fit a certain stereotype.

You don't have to be exactly like everyone else.

**You just have to be yourself and treat people with respect.**

## 🌒 Official Meetings

Clan Eclipse may hold **official group meetings and activities around school** where members can:

* Meet other Eclipse members
* Discuss upcoming activities
* Work on projects
* Share ideas
* Plan community events
* Hang out and socialize

All meetings and activities should follow **school rules and applicable policies**.
